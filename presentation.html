The Story of EmberTip

Getting paid in BCH still means pasting a raw address into a chat.

The address gets buried, amounts get typed wrong, and payers hesitate — is this really the right address? Did it arrive? Nobody can tell.

During BCH BLAZE we asked a simple question: what if a BCH address had a proper front door? A page with a name, preset amounts, a QR code, a wallet button and a live feed that shows the money landing. EmberTip is that front door.

Benefits of EmberTip

Why pages beat raw addresses

  • 30 seconds to create — name, address, amounts. No signup, ever.
  • Every wallet works — scan the BIP-21 QR, or connect with WizardConnect and sign in one tap.
  • Live receipt feed — payments appear on-chain seconds after they are sent. Trust, visible.
  • Non-custodial — funds go straight to the owner's own wallet. EmberTip never touches a key or a satoshi.
  • Unkillable pages — the whole page lives inside its own link. No server to shut down, no database to lose.
  • Free forever — static hosting costs nothing, BCH fees cost almost nothing.
BCH Usage

Real mainnet money movement

  • Mainnet transactions — every payment is a real BCH transaction: BIP-21 payments from any wallet, or transactions constructed by EmberTip and signed + broadcast by the visitor's own wallet via WizardConnect.
  • Micro-tips that actually work — at ~$0.001 fees, a 0.001 BCH tip (≈ $0.21) loses less than 0.5% to the network. Impossible on BTC or cards.
  • CashTokens — our banner carries a CashTokens address receiving EMBER2026, doubling as Community Choice voting.
  • Instant for small amounts — BCH 0-conf makes tips feel instant; the feed confirms on-chain.
